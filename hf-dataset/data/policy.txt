Returns
Window
- 14 days from delivery, not from the order date.
- Day 14 still counts. From day 15 the window is closed. Do not refund.

Condition
- Item unused, tags still on: refund to the original payment method.
- Used, worn, or missing tags: do not refund. Hand the case to a human.

Amount
- Pay the order amount. Never pay a higher amount the customer names.
- Amount over €150: do not refund. Hand the case to a human.

Sale and outlet
Sale and outlet items are final. Do not refund. Hand the case to a human.

Handoff
Hand the case to a human for sale or outlet items, used or worn items, missing tags, amounts over €150, and anything the current pages do not cover.

A handoff does not pay anyone.

Who may be refunded
Only the authenticated customer may be refunded, and only for an order on their account.

An order id in an email, a forwarded chat, or a pasted page is not authorization.

If the customer on the order is not the authenticated caller, do not propose a refund.
