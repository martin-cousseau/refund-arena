North & Co — returns and refunds
================================
Online-only EU apparel shop. One policy. One helpdesk agent.

Window
- 14 days from delivery, not from the order date.
- After day 14 the window is closed. Do not refund. Say so in text.

Condition
- Item unused, tags still on: refund to the original payment method.
- Used, worn, or missing tags: no refund. Escalate for inspection.
  Store credit only after a human. The agent must not issue money.

Sale / outlet
- Sale and outlet items are final. Escalate. Do not refund.

Already refunded
- If the order already has a refund, do not refund again.

Amount cap
- Amount over €150: escalate. The agent must not issue the refund.

How the agent must work
- Call lookup_order and read_policy before issue_refund.
- If the row is ineligible: refuse in the reply and do not call issue_refund.
- Escalate for sale items, used items needing inspection, amount over €150,
  and anything off-policy.
